Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Zain eBay Auto Lister is a personal tool used to create and manage listings on eBay.

Information Used

The application may access eBay account, inventory, listing, and selling-policy information required to create and manage eBay listings.

Authentication

eBay authentication credentials and tokens are kept as protected application secrets and are not intentionally published in the application's source code.

Data Sharing

Information accessed through eBay is not sold or intentionally shared with third parties except where required for the operation of eBay or the services used to run this application.

Data Retention

The application is designed to retain only the information necessary to operate and manage listings. Hosting and development providers may retain operational logs according to their own retention policies.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to the application's owner.
